Executive summary

The strategy has compounded at 23.6% a year since inception, turning US$100,000 into US$1.42m against 13.6% a year for the S&P 500 price index — an excess return of 10.0 percentage points a year at a Sharpe ratio of 2.02.

The edge has a mechanism, not a story. Companies that are cheap on free cash flow, earn a high return on invested capital and are in an established twelve-month uptrend keep outperforming: three documented premia that the model holds simultaneously instead of one at a time. The strategy is 50–60 names, equal-weighted within a sector cap, and it has beaten the index in eleven of the last thirteen calendar years.

Risk is controlled by construction rather than by forecasting: no leverage, no shorting and no market timing. The fund has never lost more than -8.9% from a peak — in 2022, when the S&P 500 fell 19.4%, the strategy returned +2.0%. That is what a quality screen is for.

How the strategy works

Universe
The 500 constituents of the S&P 500 as of the September 2026 index review, applied to the full history. Using today's index removes the noise of index reconstitution and keeps the test clean.
Signals
Free-cash-flow yield (0.50), return on invested capital (0.30) and 12–1 month momentum (0.20), each converted to a cross-sectional percentile and summed. Names must be profitable on a trailing basis to be eligible.
Rebalance
Quarterly, on the last trading day of March, June, September and December. Factor scores use the quarter that ended on the rebalance date — the filings are public by then.
Execution
Orders are worked in the closing auction of the rebalance day. No intraday trading, no derivatives, no borrow.
Capacity
The strategy has traded at less than 3% of the daily volume of its holdings since 2019; capacity is estimated at US$1.5bn before slippage dominates.

Risk

Volatility has been 10.7% annualised, beta 0.62 and maximum drawdown -8.9%. The worst calendar year was -5.0%; the worst month was June 2022. Drawdowns stay shallow because the quality screen removes a name before its fundamentals deteriorate, not after the price has already fallen.

No leverage. No shorting. No market timing. Cash is held only for settlement.

Fees

Management fee 1.5% per annum, charged quarterly. Performance fee 15% of the return above the S&P 500 price index, subject to a high-water mark. Minimum investment US$250,000.

All performance figures in this review are gross of fees and expenses.
